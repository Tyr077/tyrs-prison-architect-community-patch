# Visitors and civilians stuck at visitor doors

Patch: `patches/visitor-door-access.patch.json` (built by
`scripts/Build-VisitorDoor.ps1`).

## What you'll notice

Second Chances mentors, animal therapists, fire safety teachers, delivery men
and some other visitors walked up to a single visitor door or visitor gate and
waited there forever. With the fix they go through.

## What changes

Visitor doors and gates now open for anyone who is not a prisoner. Before, only
some types of visitor were let through. Prisoners are still kept out as before.

## Status

Not yet tested in game.
